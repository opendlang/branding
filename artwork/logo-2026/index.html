Design & Spacing

The logo is a grotesque sans-serif “D”-shaped glyph with an extraordinary aperture in its stem, opening its counter. Its shape is derived from the bold glyph for the latin uppercase letter “D” in the font “IBM Plex Sans”.

When padded, the “D” glyph has a slight x-offset to make it appear visually centered to the human eye.

Height: 23 or vertical padding: 2×16

X-offset from left padding: +11135
X-offset from a technically centered position: +171080
Alternatively, horizontally aligning it to the nearest 112-boundary (i.e. +191080 off-center) may also work.

Circular versions of the logo need a different x-offset in order to appear visually centered: +23240 from left padding
Circular x-offset from a technically centered position: +5144

The aperture in the stem is vertically centered and 16 in height.

All provided fractions are relative to the border-box of the logo graphic.
